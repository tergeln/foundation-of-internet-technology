Phones, laptops and watches made for every day

Lumen designs phones, laptops, watches, tablets and audio products that work together. Every Lumen phone, laptop and watch shares one account, so your photos, notes and messages follow you from one screen to the next.

The new Lumen Phone 7 is thinner and brighter, with a battery that lasts all day. Lumen Book Air is a light laptop for students and creators, and Lumen Watch keeps track of your health, sleep and workouts.

Want to save money? Trade in your old phone, laptop or watch and get credit toward a new Lumen device. You can also pay monthly with Lumen Card and get 3% back on every purchase.
